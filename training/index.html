Training & Opportunities

Building Capacity in the Himalayas

The Himalayan Langur Project runs workshops, training programmes, and conversation series that connect local field experts, students, and researchers. Our aim is to strengthen local expertise, open pathways into primatology and conservation science, and bring global perspectives into the valleys where we work.

Workshop Series

The Primate Investigators

Conversations beyond the field notes. The series connects the Himalayan Langur Project's local field experts and the broader audience with primatologists from around the world. Through two-way conversations, it explores diverse primate species, landscapes, research challenges, and community experiences, expanding local expertise, bringing global perspectives to the field, and celebrating the human stories behind the science that often remain beyond the field notes.

Hosted by Diganta Mandal, Co-Director and Field Coordinator
